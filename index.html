Getting Started

How to Play Cookie Clicker

Master the art of cookie baking in just a few clicks. Whether you're a casual player or a dedicated idle game enthusiast, Cookie Clicker is easy to learn and impossible to put down.

👆

Click the Cookie

Start by clicking the giant cookie in the center of the screen. Every click bakes cookies and adds to your all-time total. The more you click, the faster you grow!

🏗️

Buy Buildings

Spend your cookies in the Bakery Shop to purchase buildings — from humble Cursors to time-bending Portals. Each building generates cookies automatically every second.

⬆️

Unlock Upgrades

Spend cookies on upgrades that multiply your click power or supercharge your buildings. Upgrades become available as you hit cookie milestones — keep baking to unlock them all!

✨

Catch Golden Cookies

Watch for shimmering Golden Cookies that appear randomly on screen. Click them fast — they trigger powerful bonuses like Frenzy (7x production) or Cookie Storm!

🏆

Earn Achievements

Unlock 21 unique achievements as you hit baking milestones, purchase buildings, and reach click counts. Each achievement is a badge of your cookie-empire greatness.

💾

Auto-Save Progress

Your game saves automatically every 30 seconds to your browser. Even when you close the tab, your buildings keep baking — collect your offline earnings when you return!

Pro Tips

Cookie Clicker Strategy & Tips

Want to maximize your cookies per second and dominate the leaderboard? These pro strategies will accelerate your cookie empire faster than you can say "chocolate chip."

01

Prioritize Early Buildings

In the early game, buy buildings over upgrades. Grandmas and Farms compound quickly — the sooner they're running, the faster your CPS snowballs.

02

Always Buy Upgrades When Available

Upgrades give multiplicative boosts. A single upgrade that doubles your CPS is often more valuable than buying 5 more buildings of the same type.

03

Never Miss a Golden Cookie

Golden Cookies can multiply your production by 7x for 77 seconds. That's like hours of production in just over a minute. Keep an eye on screen edges!

04

Click During Frenzy

When a Frenzy Golden Cookie fires, switch to active clicking — your manual clicks are also multiplied! This is the fastest way to earn massive cookies.

05

Diversify Your Buildings

Spread purchases across building types to keep all upgrades unlocking. A balanced approach unlocks upgrades faster than max-leveling a single building.

06

Collect Offline Production

Your buildings keep working when the tab is closed. Open the game after a few hours to collect a massive cookie windfall — offline production runs up to 8 hours!

Special Events

The Golden Cookie

The most exciting moment in any Cookie Clicker session — a shimmering, glowing Golden Cookie appears and vanishes in seconds. Here's everything you need to know.

⚡

Frenzy

The most common golden cookie effect. Your entire production is multiplied by 7x for 77 seconds. This is where fortunes are made!

🍪

Cookie Storm

A rare bonus that instantly grants you a massive pile of cookies — often equivalent to several minutes or even hours of production.

💰

Lucky

Grants you a percentage of your banked cookies as a bonus reward. The bigger your bank, the bigger the Lucky payout!

🌟

Click Frenzy

Every manual click is worth 777x its normal value for a short window. Active clickers dream of this golden bonus!

💡 Pro tip: Golden Cookies appear every 3–5 minutes and last only 13 seconds. Keep the game visible on your screen for maximum catch rate!
