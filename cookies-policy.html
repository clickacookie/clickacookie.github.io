Cookies & Local Storage Policy

Understanding how Click A Cookie stores your game data — because transparency is sweet.

🍪 Wait, actual cookies or computer cookies?
Good question! In this policy, we're talking about browser storage (localStorage) — not the delicious baked goods you click in the game. But we love both kinds! 🍪

1. Do We Use Traditional Cookies?

No. Click A Cookie does not use traditional HTTP cookies for tracking, analytics, or advertising. We believe in minimal data storage and maximum privacy.

Instead, we use localStorage — a modern web storage technology that saves data directly in your browser. This is what allows your game progress to be saved between sessions.

2. What Is localStorage?

localStorage is a feature built into every modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge, etc.). It allows websites to store small amounts of data on your device. Unlike cookies, localStorage data is not sent to our servers — it stays entirely on your computer or phone.

3. What Data Does Click A Cookie Store?

When you play Click A Cookie, we store the following information in your browser's localStorage:

Cookie CountYour current number of cookiesSave your progress
Buildings OwnedHow many of each building you have purchasedSave your automation setup
Upgrades PurchasedWhich upgrades you have unlockedSave your click/building multipliers

Important: We do not store your name, email address, IP address, or any personally identifiable information in localStorage.

4. How Long Is Data Stored?

Your game data remains in localStorage indefinitely until you or your browser removes it. The data persists even after you close your browser or restart your computer.

Data is only removed when:

  • You click the "Reset Game" button inside Click A Cookie
  • You manually clear your browser's localStorage (via browser settings)
  • You use private/incognito mode (data is cleared when you close the window)
  • Your browser automatically clears storage due to low space (rare)

5. Can I Control or Delete My Data?

Absolutely! You have complete control over your game data. Here's how:

Browser-specific instructions:

  • Chrome/Edge: Settings → Privacy & Security → Clear browsing data → Advanced → Select "Local storage" → Clear data
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies & Site Data → Clear Data → Uncheck "Cookies" but check "Site Data" → Clear
  • Safari: Develop → Empty Caches (or Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data → Remove)
💡 Pro Tip: If you want to start fresh without losing your current save, you can play in an incognito/private window. Your main save will remain untouched!

6. Does Click A Cookie Use Third-Party Cookies?

No. We do not integrate with:

  • ❌ Advertising networks
  • ❌ Social media pixels (Facebook, Twitter, etc.)
  • ❌ Google Analytics (standard version)
  • ❌ Any third-party tracking cookies

Our only external connections are to:

  • ✅ Google Fonts (for the Fredoka One and Nunito fonts)
  • ✅ GitHub Pages (where the game is hosted)

These services may set their own cookies. Please refer to Google's Cookie Policy and GitHub's Privacy Statement for details.

7. What About "Cookie" Clicking in the Game?

Just for fun — every time you click the giant cookie in our game, it's a literal cookie that you're clicking! 🍪 These clicks do not create browser cookies. They create virtual cookies that exist only within the game. Your browser doesn't store anything about each individual click.

8. Offline Production & LocalStorage

Offline production works by saving a timestamp when you last played. When you return, the game calculates how many cookies your buildings produced based on the time difference. This calculation uses data from localStorage. If localStorage is cleared, your offline progress cannot be calculated.

9. Consent

By playing Click A Cookie, you consent to the use of localStorage as described in this policy. You are not required to accept traditional tracking cookies because we don't use them.

If you prefer not to have any data stored, you can play in private/incognito mode. Your progress won't be saved between sessions, but the game will still work perfectly.

We may update this Cookies Policy occasionally. The "Last Updated" date at the top indicates when changes were made. Continued use of Click A Cookie after changes constitutes acceptance.
