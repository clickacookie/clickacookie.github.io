Roberto is the creator and driving force behind Click A Cookie. What started as a late-night coding session fueled by actual cookies has grown into a beloved idle game played by thousands worldwide. Roberto believes that games should be fun, free, and accessible to everyone — no paywalls, no ads, just pure cookie-clicking joy.

📖 The Story Behind the Game

Click A Cookie was born from a simple idea: what if clicking a cookie could be the most satisfying thing on the internet? As a lifelong fan of incremental games, Roberto wanted to create something that captured the magic of watching numbers grow while adding his own creative twist. The result is a game that's easy to pick up but impossible to put down — from your very first click to building an interdimensional cookie empire.

"I wanted to build a game that brings a smile to people's faces. No stress, no pressure — just you, a giant cookie, and the satisfying feeling of watching your cookie count soar. Click A Cookie is my gift to the idle game community."
— Roberto, Founder of Click A Cookie

⏰ The Journey So Far

2024

Roberto starts developing Click A Cookie as a passion project, inspired by classic idle games and a love for baking.

2025

The first version launches with 8 buildings and 10 upgrades. Players from around the world discover the joy of clicking the giant cookie.

2026

Click A Cookie expands to 14 buildings, 20+ upgrades, 21 achievements, and introduces Golden Cookie events. The game reaches thousands of daily players!
