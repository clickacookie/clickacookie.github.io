Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 10, 2026

Welcome to Click A Cookie! We value your privacy and are committed to being transparent about our data practices. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you visit and play our free online cookie clicker game at clickacookie.github.io.

📢 Quick Summary: Click A Cookie does NOT collect personal information like your name, email, or address. Your game progress is saved locally in your browser. We use minimal analytics to understand game usage patterns and improve the experience. No data is sold or shared with third parties for advertising.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

We do not require you to create an account or provide personal information to play Click A Cookie. You can play completely anonymously.

1.2 Automatically Collected Information

When you visit our website, certain standard information may be collected automatically:

  • Game Progress Data: Your cookie count, buildings owned, upgrades purchased, and achievements are stored locally in your browser's localStorage. This data never leaves your device unless you manually export it.
  • Usage Analytics: We may use privacy-friendly analytics tools (like simple page view counters) to understand how many people play the game, which features are most popular, and general geographic regions (country level only).
  • Technical Information: Your browser type, operating system, screen resolution, and language preference — collected only for optimizing game performance and appearance.

1.3 Cookies & Local Storage

Click A Cookie uses localStorage (not traditional cookies) to save your game progress. This data stays on your device and is never transmitted to our servers unless you manually share it. We do not use tracking cookies for cross-site advertising or behavioral profiling.

Storage Type Purpose Duration
localStorage Save game progress (cookies, buildings, upgrades) Until cleared by user
Session Storage Temporary game state during active session Until browser tab is closed

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the limited information we collect solely to:

  • 🍪 Save and restore your game progress — so you never lose your cookie empire.
  • 📊 Improve the game experience — understanding which buildings and upgrades players enjoy most helps us balance the game.
  • 🛠️ Fix bugs and optimize performance — technical data helps us ensure the game runs smoothly on all devices.
  • 📈 Count visitors — we like knowing that people enjoy our game!

3. Third-Party Services

Click A Cookie does not use third-party advertising networks, tracking pixels, or social media widgets that collect personal data. We do not integrate with Facebook, Google Analytics (standard version), or other common tracking tools.

Hosting: Our website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect standard server logs (IP addresses, request timestamps) as described in GitHub's Privacy Policy. This is beyond our control.

4. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, rent, or share your personal information with third parties. The only exceptions (extremely rare) would be:

  • To comply with legal obligations (court order, law enforcement request).
  • To protect the rights, property, or safety of Click A Cookie, our users, or the public.

Since we don't collect personal information, these scenarios are largely theoretical.

5. Children's Privacy

Click A Cookie is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13. We do not require registration, email addresses, or any identifying information. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us, and we will take appropriate steps.

6. Your Choices & Rights

Because we don't collect personal information, there's no database of your data to delete or access. However, you have complete control over your game save data:

  • 🗑️ Delete your game data: Use the reset button in the game, or clear your browser's localStorage (Settings → Privacy → Clear Site Data).
  • 📤 Export your save: Your game progress is stored locally and can be backed up if you wish.
  • 🚫 Opt-out of analytics: Since our analytics are minimal and anonymous, no opt-out is necessary.
🔐 Your Data Stays With You: Unlike most online games, your cookie empire lives only on your device. We don't have servers storing your progress. This means you're always in control.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information we collect. Since your game data never leaves your browser, the primary security risk is unauthorized access to your device. We recommend keeping your browser and operating system updated.

8. International Data Transfers

Our website is hosted globally via GitHub's CDN. Your connection to our site may route through servers in various countries. However, because we don't collect personal data, international transfer concerns do not apply.
